## Field Guide

**name** *(required)*: Campaign name — e.g. `Summer Sale 2026 — DE`
**description**: Campaign objective and target audience
**market**: Target market(s) / region(s) — e.g. `DE`, `DE, AT`, `EU`
**phase**: Current phase — `Briefing` | `Concept` | `Production` | `Review` | `Launch` | `Reporting`
**go_live**: Campaign launch date — e.g. `"2026-07-01"`
**budget**: Campaign budget (optional)
**notes**: Agency, channel strategy, legal/compliance notes
